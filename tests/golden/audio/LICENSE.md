# Audio DSP golden vector license

SPDX-License-Identifier: CC0-1.0

The PCM fixture definitions, deterministic generators, generated analysis vectors,
and generated test timbres in this directory are dedicated to the public domain
under CC0 1.0.

Every waveform is constructed by project-authored integer formulas. No recording,
user media, `package/` content, or third-party audiovisual work is an input. The
three `AT-*` fixtures are test-only timbres and are not approved product sounds.
